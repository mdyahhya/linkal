Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need any technical knowledge to build my store?
Absolutely not. linkal.in is designed for Indian small business owners — not developers. If you can use WhatsApp and Instagram, you can build your store. Everything is point-and-click: upload photos, type names and prices, pick a theme, and hit publish. Most sellers go live in under 10 minutes.
Can my customers pay online through the store?
linkal.in uses WhatsApp-based ordering — customers tap "Order on WhatsApp," get sent to a pre-filled message, and you handle payment your preferred way (UPI, bank transfer, cash on delivery). This keeps things simple and builds trust. Online payment gateway integration is coming in a future update for Pro plan users.
What happens after I sign up?
As soon as you sign up, our team contacts you on WhatsApp within 2 hours to help you set up your store. Your subdomain (yourname.linkal.in) is activated immediately. We also send you a step-by-step setup guide on WhatsApp so you can get your first product listed in minutes.
Can I change my store design anytime?
Yes — switch themes, change your logo, update your tagline, or add a new banner anytime from your dashboard. Changes go live instantly. You can even set up seasonal themes for festivals manually or let Auto Festival Mode handle it for you on Growth and Pro plans.
How does the AI Business Analyzer work?
Every week (or daily on Pro), our AI scans your store's traffic data, product click rates, WhatsApp order taps, and pricing — and compares it against trending data in your category and region. It then generates a personalized report delivered to your WhatsApp with specific, actionable suggestions: what to fix, what to promote, and what to add. Think of it as having a digital marketing advisor available 24/7 at no extra cost.
What if I want to cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime — no lock-in contracts, no cancellation fees. Just message us on WhatsApp or email us, and we'll process your cancellation immediately. Your store stays live until the end of your current billing period, then goes offline. Your data is kept for 30 days in case you want to reactivate.
